# Kura tagger overview (main f9c05fe0 / source 779e4cf6): adversarial evaluation

**Verdict: 1 pass, 8 partial, 1 fail. This is not a full approval, and not a quality approval of the candidate.** The new first fold works on desktop: the request names the tag and has a direct 159-image link above the fold at 1280. It is weaker on a phone, and duplication got worse in the request area.

I checked the four screenshots, the pasted text and the three component sources. I did not exercise any interaction, so the following are unverified:
- opened graph drawers
- link destinations (`/?q=…`)
- error and stale states
- keyboard traversal
- the embedded iframe
- the scope of the parent's `詳細集計を再読み込み` button

## Holding up under attack

- The new request summary: tag named, direct link, and the 159-image row has no comparison numbers or scores that could bias the judge.
- Hold names are inline.
- The machine-scoring label and the bottom caveat (`不一致は人の誤りとは限りません…`) avoid claiming quality or that human labels are wrong.

## The one next small improvement

**Lift the `あなたの確認待ち` banner to the top of the overview, above `本番と改善処理`, with the completion condition in its note.**

The human task is what this page exists to surface, and at 390 it is currently the last thing in the first screen. The banner is a few lines of markup inside `.summary`; it can be rendered first as its own small block sharing the same queue fetch, with the table staying where it is. If that slot then also drops row 1's duplicate link, it removes the worst part of the duplication fail.
