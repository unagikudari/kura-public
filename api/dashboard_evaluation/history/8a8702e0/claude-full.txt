## The findings that matter

- **Completion rule contradicts itself.** 「分からない画像は残して構いません」 is followed directly by 「残る間は評価も保留です」, and the details add 「未判定が残る間は全体の品質を確定しません」. You cannot tell whether skipping ambiguous images blocks the candidate indefinitely or is acceptable.
- **0/341 is unexplained.** The table shows a smegma fixed set with 160 already judged, yet the request shows 0/341. Nothing says the 341 are the frozen 159 plus 182 extension images and that all need fresh explicit labels. It reads as lost work or a counting bug.
- **Experiment phase label is wrong for the current run.** The line reads 「未公開 · 機械による候補の採点」 while the host field carries 「nodeb CPU · 本番と同じ6GiB枠で検証」. Scoring is finished; what is running is the resource qualification. The page implies quality scoring is still in progress and never says this run is separate from quality.
- **Two smegma lines look contradictory.** The experiment shows as running, and the next line says smegma 「現在の稼働は未確認」. They are different processes, but both are labelled only with the tag.
- **「比較はまだ表示しません」 gives no reason.** Without "to avoid biasing your judgment" it reads as broken or pending.

## The one next improvement

Replace the two notes under the request card with a single one that states the rule and explains the zero:

> 341枚すべてに新しく判定を付けます（過去の判定は引き継ぎません）。迷う画像は未判定のまま残せます。「なし」扱いにはならず、残っている間は評価が保留になります。

It answers "why 0?" and "must I finish all?" in the same space, and removes one duplicated caveat. It is a text-only change in the request section. The middle clause, 「過去の判定は引き継ぎません」, is my reading of "all new explicit labels needed"; confirm it before shipping.

The experiment phase label is the next candidate after this, since it needs a new phase or label from the backend. I would stop after the note change rather than continue polishing the low-priority items.
